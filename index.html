Bay Honors Research Conference

Bay Honors Research Conference

Worked with Professor Liu from Irvine Valley College to explore the representation of sexualities in erotic fanfiction. Qualitatively coded through over 800,000 words of fanfiction to analyze narrative patterns and themes. Choosen among over 250+ applicants to present by the Bay Honors Consortium. Presented my original researchs findings in a 15 minute oral presentation to over 30 students and professors. Continued to refine my research over the summer to submit a paper for publication.

Presented at University of California, Berkeley in April 2025. Published in the ThinkYou?! Journal in November 2025.
